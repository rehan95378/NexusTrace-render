=== REPORT 6 ===
A final name surfaced from a traffic camera near the same block: Vikram Nair, briefly seen in Rahul Sharma's company outside a currency exchange. Rahul Sharma was seen with Vikram Nair the same week the Deepak Rao and Farhan Ali sightings were logged. Vikram Nair has not been connected to any other individual, vehicle, or transaction in the case file.
